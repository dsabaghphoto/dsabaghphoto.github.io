About

Chef.
Photographer.
Explorer.

I'm David — a Spanish photographer based in Bristol with 10 years of experience in the hospitality industry. Born in Chipiona, Cádiz. My background as a professional chef gives me a unique eye for food: I don't just photograph dishes, I understand them.

From the depths of the ocean to the wild landscapes of Andalucía, I chase light, texture, and the moments between moments. Available for commercial, editorial and personal projects worldwide.
